# Scenario Image Suggestions

Use these as generation targets for new scenario backgrounds. Keep them 16:9, cinematic, no text, no logos, and visually readable behind the game UI.

## Priority Backgrounds

1. `contested-resource-hearing.png`
   - Public hearing inside a ruined civic hall, rival survivor factions around a table, water cans and ration ledgers visible, tense torchlight.

2. `failed-rescue-witnesses.png`
   - Collapsed overpass rescue scene at dusk, survivors watching from both sides, improvised stretchers, a hard moral decision in progress.

3. `sabotaged-infrastructure-repair.png`
   - Broken aqueduct or floodgate repair site, mechanics with scavenged tools, armed guards, water threatening one settlement below.

4. `public-reckoning-ritual.png`
   - Post-collapse trial or oath ceremony in a repurposed school gym, candles, faction banners, accused figure at center without gore.

5. `threatened-fog-market.png`
   - Foggy barter market using colored cloth signals, traders reaching for weapons, child or messenger holding forbidden colors.

6. `limited-evacuation-convoy.png`
   - Evacuation convoy with too few seats, families, fuel carriers, scouts, and a blocked winter or desert route ahead.

7. `quarantine-triage-dispute.png`
   - Makeshift clinic in a bunker or train station, quarantine line, medics arguing over scarce medicine, hopeful but strained lighting.

8. `misinformation-radio-room.png`
   - Damaged radio room crowded with anxious listeners, accused broadcaster, maps pinned to walls, storm light through cracked windows.

9. `shelter-leadership-vote.png`
   - Underground shelter vote during a power failure, raised hands or tokens, leaders split by candlelight, leaking ceiling pipes.

10. `refugee-border-crossing.png`
    - Improvised border checkpoint between settlements, refugees carrying bundles, guards at barricades, uncertain dawn.

11. `unclear-salvage-claim.png`
    - Salvage yard around a half-buried supply truck, multiple groups claiming ownership, visible tools, crates, and old markings.

12. `weather-hidden-loyalties.png`
    - Mountain storm or flash flood forcing enemies into the same shelter, silhouettes arguing while the disaster closes in.

## Visual Coverage

These fill gaps in the current set, which is strong on broad locations but light on specific social scenes. Prioritize images with visible objects of conflict: ledgers, water valves, flags, radios, medicine, fuel, gates, ballots, and convoy seats.
